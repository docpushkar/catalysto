FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from manufacturing businesses about working with Catalysto.

Do you work with SMEs and smaller manufacturing businesses?
+
Yes. Catalysto works with manufacturing businesses of all sizes — from growing SMEs looking to build their first operational systems, to large multi-plant operations. We tailor our approach to fit the size, budget and operational maturity of each business.
Do you provide on-site factory assessments?
+
Yes. Most of our engagements begin with an on-site visit to understand the factory environment, workforce structure and current operational challenges. We believe the best systems are built from a deep understanding of the real factory floor — not assumptions.
Which industries do you work with?
+
We work across a wide range of manufacturing sectors including automotive, engineering, industrial manufacturing, process industries, heavy engineering, packaging and general production facilities. If your business operates a factory or plant, we can help.
Do you provide workforce productivity systems?
+
Yes. Workforce productivity tracking is a core part of our PerformanceOS vertical. We build dashboards, shift analytics and reporting systems that give managers and supervisors real-time visibility into workforce performance at the department and operator level.
What is your typical project timeline?
+
Timelines vary based on the scope and complexity of the engagement. A focused content project (e.g. SOP videos) can be completed in 3–6 weeks. A full PerformanceOS implementation typically takes 6–12 weeks. We discuss timelines clearly during the initial consultation.
Can Catalysto help with ergonomic assessments?
+
Yes. Our Health+ vertical focuses entirely on workforce ergonomics and sustainability. We conduct on-site ergonomic assessments, identify high-risk workstations and develop practical intervention programs — including fatigue reduction, movement optimisation and workstation redesign.
